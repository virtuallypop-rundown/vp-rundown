Artist development

Sony Music UK A&R Academy applications close 7 December

Sony Music UK is recruiting three interns for its 2027 A&R Academy, a 12-month, full-time programme across RCA, Columbia Records and Ministry of Sound at its King's Cross office in London. It starts in mid-March 2027. Applications need a written application and a video cover letter, and close at 10am on 7 December. Applicants must have the right to work in the UK.

Why it matters: Paid routes into major-label A&R are rare. This is worth passing on to the young scouts, promoters and managers in your network, who are often the ones spotting new acts first.

Sources: Sony Music UK

Updates

Entertainment law

Disney sued over unlicensed music in social promos

On 24 November, publisher LAA Music and its owner Zac Hiller sued Disney in a Michigan federal court. They claim three of their songs were used without a licence in seven promotional social videos for ESPN, Hulu, FX and Disney Parks, and say Disney didn't produce a licence after being contacted in August. Disney hasn't responded publicly, and the claims are untested. Separately, on 23 November an Ohio judge turned down a procedural bid by DSW's owner to drop 75 social posts from Warner Music's copyright case over music in brand and influencer videos, so those posts stay in the case.

Why it matters: Both cases concern music added to brand content on Instagram and TikTok. Reports note that the platforms' in-app music libraries are generally licensed for personal rather than commercial use. Anyone running promo accounts for tours or venues may want to review how the music on their posts is cleared.

Sources: Digital Music News (Disney) · Digital Music News (DSW)

Music marketing & social

Tidal moves to ban fully AI-generated tracks

Tidal has updated its AI policy to ban tracks that are fully AI-generated. In June it had said it would accept them, tag them and pay them no royalties.

Why it matters: Streaming services are taking different approaches to AI music, from labelling to outright bans. Independent releases that use AI tools should be clear about how much of the work is human-made, so they don't get caught by platform filters.

Sources: Music Ally

Wider context

Live music & touring

Viagogo-funded study adds to CMA investigation calls

Research by the Centre for Economics and Business Research (Cebr), commissioned by resale platform viagogo, says UK concert ticket prices have more than doubled since 2010, rising much faster than general inflation. It also says Ticketmaster UK's 2025 pre-tax profit was higher than the estimated combined profit of all UK grassroots venues. Cebr is calling for a full Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) market investigation into Live Nation's position, echoing calls from MPs on the business committee.

Why it matters: The report comes from a resale platform that is itself facing provisional CMA findings on its fees, so read it with that in mind. Even so, it keeps primary ticket pricing and market concentration on the political agenda alongside the resale cap.

Sources: Business Wire via AP

Artist development

Even adds on-demand vinyl to artist stores

The direct-to-fan platform Even has added a Day & Date Vinyl service to its white-label Even Studio tools. Artists can sell vinyl editions of new and catalogue albums from their own websites, and Even says there are no upfront costs, minimum order quantities or stock risk.

Why it matters: It's a low-risk way to test demand for vinyl. That could suit catalogue projects, live albums or merch tables without committing to a pressing up front.

Sources: Music Ally
