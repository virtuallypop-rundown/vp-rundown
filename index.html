Weekend edition

Sunday 15 November 2026

A quieter weekend, led by new TikTok figures on UK music livestreams and a desktop version of Instagram's Edits app.

Updates

Music marketing & social

TikTok says UK musicians' livestreams now average 4.9m views a day

TikTok has published its first UK figures for music on TikTok LIVE: more than 46,000 UK music creators went live between January and mid-October 2026, and in September their streams averaged 4.9 million global views a day, up 74% on a year earlier.

Why it matters: Live streams give acts a way to turn short-clip discovery into a regular audience that can be pointed towards tour dates, merch and new releases. These are TikTok's own figures, so treat them as a guide rather than an independent measure.

Sources: TikTok Newsroom · Music Week · Music Ally

Music marketing & social

Instagram's Edits video app arrives on desktop

Meta's Edits video editor, previously phone-only, now has a Mac version with a multi-track timeline, keyframes, watermark-free 4K export and direct posting to Instagram, with caption and text styles synced to the mobile app. Instagram has also added an AI Effect Builder for creating Stories effects from a text description.

Why it matters: Teams cutting tour trailers and Reels can now edit on a computer and publish straight to Instagram without moving files to a phone. Custom Stories effects link back to the creator's profile, which could offer a small extra route to discovery.

Sources: Music Ally · JCK

Entertainment law

Alice Merton's sample claim against Ye is settled

German singer-songwriter Alice Merton's US copyright lawsuit against Ye (formerly Kanye West), which alleged her song 'Blindside' was sampled in 'Gun to My Head' after she refused clearance, has been dismissed following a settlement. The terms were not disclosed.

Why it matters: It's another reminder that using a sample without clearance can lead to a lawsuit, even when the dispute ends in a private settlement. The same applies to recordings used in show intros and promotional videos.

Sources: Digital Music News

Coverage note: A quiet weekend for UK touring and ticketing: the main live story, the Cultural Exchange Coalition's letter ahead of the 20 November UK-EU summit, was covered on Friday. Several legal stories circulating this weekend (Sony and DSW's settlement, the second UMG and Sony suit against Suno) were older developments and were left out.
