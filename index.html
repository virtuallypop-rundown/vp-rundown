Weekend edition

Sunday 22 November 2026

A quiet Sunday, so this edition focuses on two practical opportunities: Arts Council England's Music Growth Fund deadlines and Sound City's call for artists to play Liverpool in 2027.

Needs attention

Artist development

Music Growth Fund: smaller round closes 26 November

Arts Council England's Music Growth Fund round for £1,000 to £40,000 closes at 11.59pm on Thursday 26 November, with the next round opening on 27 November. The larger £40,001 to £100,000 strand opens a new round tomorrow, 23 November, closing on 15 January 2027.

Why it matters: The fund is aimed at music businesses rather than individual artists, including promoters, managers, labels, studios, festivals and venues developing new UK music, so it could back a promoter's new-music programme or artist campaign. Check the match-funding rules before applying.

Sources: Sound and Music · Action Together

Artist development

Sound City opens 2027 Apply to Play

Liverpool's Sound City festival for new music is taking applications from emerging artists for its 2027 edition, held in the city centre on 1-2 May. Applications close at 9pm on Friday 29 January 2027.

Why it matters: It's a well-known industry showcase on the doorstep for Merseyside acts, and developing artists you work with can apply now without rushing against a deadline.

Sources: Sound City · Neon Music

Coverage note: Quiet weekend for touring, platform and legal news. The Music Growth Fund dates come from Sound and Music and Action Together, which now agree; Arts Council England's own funding page could not be reached for confirmation, so check the Arts Council portal before applying. Sound City's call opened earlier this month but had not been covered in previous editions.
